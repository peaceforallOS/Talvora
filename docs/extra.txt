================================================================================
VIDEO CALL APPLICATION — ADDITIONAL FEATURES (extra.txt)
================================================================================

In accordance with instruction 8 of the project specifications, the following
extra features have been added to the application to enhance usability, 
security, performance, and overall user experience while keeping the system 
simple and elegant:

1. Real-time Audio Level & Speaking Indicators
   - Description: Active speakers are highlighted with an animated visual audio 
     meter on their video tile and participant list item. This helps room members
     immediately identify who is speaking, even in larger calls or when cameras are 
     turned off.

2. Screen Sharing Capability
   - Description: Participants can share their entire desktop screen or a specific 
     application window with other room participants through the relay server. 
     The screen share stream is seamlessly integrated into the video grid layout.

3. Live Connection Quality & Latency Diagnostics
   - Description: Real-time network statistics indicator (RTT/ping, packet loss, 
     and relay connection status) displayed unobtrusively in the status bar so 
     users can diagnose poor local network conditions.

4. Chat Message Delivery & Timestamp Formatting
   - Description: All chat messages (public, direct, and group) feature localized 
     timestamps, delivery acknowledgments, and clear visual separation with 
     distinct badges distinguishing Public, Private (1-to-1), and Group messages.

5. Client Device Selector & Live Previews
   - Description: Before or during a call, users can easily select their desired 
     camera and microphone from a clean settings dropdown, featuring a live mic test 
     meter and video preview window.

6. Synchronized Music Playback Progress & Scrubbing
   - Description: When a user plays music, playback timing and track progress are 
     continuously synchronized across all clients so all participants hear the audio 
     in sync. The active DJ or Admin can seek/scrub through the track.

7. Search & Filter in Participant Directory
   - Description: A quick search bar within the user management panel and in-app 
     participant list allows instant lookup of users in large rooms to easily manage 
     permissions or initiate 1-to-1 chats.
